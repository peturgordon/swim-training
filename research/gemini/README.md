# Gemini research export

`gemini-masters-swim-training-framework.pdf` is the raw export from an independent Google Gemini research session, given the same masters-swim-training brief as this project, kept here for posterity.

**Most of it doesn't hold up.** See [Second Opinion](second-opinion.html) for the full critique: of its 56 citations, roughly 4 are peer-reviewed or PMC-indexed — the rest are coaching blogs, brand marketing, forum posts, a student presentation, and (genuinely) a human breathing-mechanics claim sourced to an *Equine Veterinary Journal* article about horses. Several of its most confident, specific recommendations (a precise periodization macrocycle, a specific fix for cardiac drift, "muscle memory outpaces fitness") sit in exactly the areas this project's own adversarially-verified research found to be unresolved or refuted.

Kept as a reference point, not as a source to draw claims from.
